About Us

Why this platform was created

Our Mission

The Maldives is a beautiful country, but many people here feel lonely — especially those who are new, living alone for work, or older adults who are far from family.

Connect Maldives was created so these people can safely meet each other and reduce feelings of loneliness.

Who Can Join?

  • Anyone living in the Maldives (Maldivian or foreigner)
  • People aged 18 years or older

What We Are Not

  • This is not a dating site
  • We do not encourage any financial transactions
  • We never sell or share your personal information
