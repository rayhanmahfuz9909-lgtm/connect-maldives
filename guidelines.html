Safety Guidelines

Please follow these rules to keep the community safe for everyone

1. Respect & Kindness

  • Treat everyone with respect.
  • Harassment, insults, or offensive behaviour is not allowed.
  • No discrimination based on religion, nationality, gender or any other reason.

2. Meeting in Person

  • Meet for the first time in a public place (café, park, market).
  • Tell a family member or friend where you are going.
  • Avoid meeting alone late at night.

3. Personal Information

  • Do not share your ID, passport or bank details with anyone.
  • Be careful about sharing your home address or workplace details.

4. Money & Transactions

  • This platform is not for money lending or business deals.
  • If someone asks for money, be careful and report them.

5. Report Problems

If anyone makes you uncomfortable, harasses you, or breaks the rules — please report them. We will take action quickly.

6. Age Requirement

This platform is only for people aged 18 and above.
